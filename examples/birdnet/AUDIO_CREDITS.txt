Why this fixture: the previous cardinal.wav was not a Northern Cardinal.
BirdNET scores it 0.0096 for Northern Cardinal and calls it Pyrrhuloxia at
0.66, while it scores three independent ground-truth cardinal recordings
0.38 / 0.70 / 0.84. Every stage of this example -- TFLite reference, psp-tc
codegen, device -- agreed with each other on that file; they were faithfully
reproducing a wrong answer, so nothing downstream could have caught it.
On this fixture the model returns Northern Cardinal at 0.84, with House
Sparrow second, matching the recordist's own notes.
